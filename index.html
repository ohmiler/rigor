RIGOR

One night. One street. Get to the extraction point alive.

  • WASD move
  • Mouse aim
  • Left click fire
  • R reload
  • Right click shove
  • 1 / 2 / 3 / Q rifle / pistol / knife / last
  • G throw a bottle
  • Shift sprint
  • C walk quietly
  • Space vault or climb
  • Space mash to break free
  • F flashlight
  • V visibility
  • P pause
  • M sound on/off
  • Wheel zoom

Stand still to shoot straight; on the run your shots spray.
Gunfire carries. Every zombie that hears it comes looking.
Climb onto a car to get out of reach. They will follow, slowly.
Shoot the pale ones before they scream. Keep away from the big ones' fists.
Shoot a leg and it hobbles; shoot again and it crawls. Shoot off both arms and it can't grab.
A knife is silent: take one from behind. Grabbed? Stab it.
A smashed bottle draws the ones that haven't seen you.

Or press Enter to play in a window. Esc leaves fullscreen.
